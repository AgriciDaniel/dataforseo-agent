# Rate limits and error codes

#confidence/evidence-based

Paraphrase: an account may make up to 10000 calls per minute (40202 beyond) and 30 simultaneous requests (40209 beyond). Endpoint exceptions include Google Ads Live at 12 per minute and tasks_ready at 20 per minute. Errored tasks are not charged.
