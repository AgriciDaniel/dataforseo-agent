# Publishing notice

This brain ships inside the DataForSEO Agent. It is not published as a website.

## Rights boundary
DataForSEO documentation, pricing pages and API responses belong to DataForSEO. The brain stores dated
paraphrases and short attributed quotes for internal use. Do not republish captured docs or responses.

## What can be public
- The wiki notes under `wiki/` (paraphrased, source-cited operating knowledge).
- `SKILL.md`, `docs/`, `references/CONFIDENCE_TAGS.md`, `references/canon/` (paraphrases with source links).
- `references/claim-ledger.md` and `references/source-ledger.json`, reviewed 2026-11-16: they hold
  paraphrased claims and links to public DataForSEO pages, no captured pages or responses.
- The sample `.raw/` fixtures under `assets/template-brain/` and `examples/sample-vault/`, which are
  written for the demo and contain no captured material.

## What must stay private
- `.raw/` in a working brain (captured docs pages and live API response fixtures).
- Other source evidence and working files, unless reviewed first.
- `_review/` (audit outputs and archived material).
- Any credentials, account data, balances or client data. None should exist in the brain; if found, remove them.
