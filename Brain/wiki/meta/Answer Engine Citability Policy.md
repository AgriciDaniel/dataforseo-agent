# Answer Engine Citability Policy

**Operating contract:** answers the agent gives about DataForSEO cite the endpoint path and, for prices or limits, the official page and date.

- Name the exact endpoint path and mode when recommending a call.
- When quoting a price or limit, give the catalog figure and the pricing or docs page it came from, with its retrieval date.
- Label estimates (Labs traffic, clickstream volume, spam score) as estimates.
- Keep answers short and scannable: endpoint, why, cost estimate, next step.

Every note this policy governs carries a confidence tag (see [[CONVENTIONS]]).
