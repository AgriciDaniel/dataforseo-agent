# Corpus Scope Policy

**Operating contract:** the brain covers the DataForSEO v3 API and how to use it well; nothing else is stored here.

## In scope
- DataForSEO APIs, endpoints, execution modes, billing, limits, errors, sandbox, MCP server, and workflows built on them.
- Comparison context with other SEO data vendors, only inside [[dataforseo-vs-ahrefs-semrush-moz]] and [[when-not-to-use-dataforseo]].

## Out of scope
- How-to content for other SEO tools, general SEO theory not tied to a DataForSEO call, personal or machine-specific setup, product marketing.
- Out-of-scope material found during maintenance moves to `_review/archive/`.

Every note this policy governs carries a confidence tag (see [[CONVENTIONS]]).
