# Routing by surface

When the user names a place (Google, Amazon, ChatGPT...) rather than a job, find the modules here. Detail notes are under `reference/surfaces/`.

| Surface | Live or queued results | Pre-indexed / analytics | Detail |
|---|---|---|---|
| Google Search | [[serp]] organic, AI Mode, AI Overview, News, Images, Jobs, Autocomplete, Ads, Finance | [[labs]] keywords and competitors; [[keywords-data]] Google Ads volume | [[plat-google-search]] |
| Google Maps and local | [[serp]] Maps, Local Finder | [[business-data]] profiles, reviews, Q&A, hotels, Business Listings | [[plat-google-maps-local]] |
| Bing | [[serp]] Bing organic | [[keywords-data]] Bing Ads | [[plat-bing-search]] |
| YouTube | [[serp]] YouTube organic, video info, comments, subtitles | none | [[plat-youtube]] |
| Baidu, Yahoo, Seznam, Naver | [[serp]] (Baidu and Seznam queue only) | none | [[plat-other-search-engines]] |
| Amazon | [[merchant]] products, ASIN, sellers | [[labs]] Amazon keywords and competitors | [[plat-amazon-marketplace]] |
| Google Shopping | [[merchant]] products, info, sellers, reviews (queue only) | none | [[plat-google-shopping]] |
| App Store, Google Play | [[app-data]] searches, lists, info, reviews, listings | [[labs]] app keywords and competitors | [[plat-app-stores]] |
| ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity, AI Overview | [[ai-optimization]] LLM Responses, LLM Scraper | [[ai-optimization]] LLM Mentions, AI keyword volume | [[plat-ai-assistants]] |
| Trustpilot, Tripadvisor, Google reviews, social counts | [[business-data]] | [[content-analysis]] for web-wide sentiment | [[plat-review-platforms]] |
| Any website (technical) | [[onpage]] crawl, Instant Pages, Lighthouse | [[domain-analytics]] technologies, Whois; [[backlinks]] | |
